The Journey

Civil first in London, then everyone travels to Sri Lanka for three days of activities, the Hindu ceremony, and the Catholic ceremony & reception the next day.
Step 1 · London

Civil Wedding

The legal marriage, a few months ahead. Intimate, at a London register office or licensed venue.

Step 2 · Sri Lanka

Guests Arrive

3 days of activities as guests land on different days — welcome dinner, sightseeing, beach & a mehndi/sangeet night.

Step 3 · Day A

Hindu Ceremony

Held between a breakfast and a meal — muhurtham, poruwa/manavarai, thali & blessings.

Step 4 · Day B

Catholic Ceremony

Church wedding the next day, followed by the grand reception.

Step 5 · Finale

Reception — with a bang

Dinner, dancing, speeches and a show-stopping send-off to end it all.
